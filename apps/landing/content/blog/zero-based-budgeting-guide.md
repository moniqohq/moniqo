Zero-based budgeting is a method where you assign every single dollar of income a specific job before the month begins — so your income minus your expenses equals exactly zero.

## What does "zero-based" actually mean?

It doesn't mean your bank account hits zero. It means your budget does. Every rupee you earn gets assigned a purpose: rent, groceries, savings, fun money. When income minus all assignments equals zero, you have a zero-based budget.

## Why it works

When you force every dollar to have a purpose, you think twice before spending. You're no longer vaguely "trying to save" — you have a concrete plan, and deviating from it is a conscious choice.

## Getting started

1. List your monthly income
2. List every expense category you can think of
3. Assign every rupee until you reach zero
4. Adjust envelopes as life happens

It sounds simple because it is. The difficulty is in the discipline, and that's exactly what Moniqo is designed to support.
