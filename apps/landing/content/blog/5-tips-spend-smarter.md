You don't need to overhaul your entire life to spend smarter. Small, consistent changes compound over time into real financial freedom.

## 1. Review your envelopes weekly, not monthly

Most people set a budget at the start of the month and never look at it again. Check in weekly — five minutes every Sunday is enough to catch overspending before it snowballs.

## 2. Give every impulse buy a 48-hour wait

Before buying anything unplanned above a small threshold, wait 48 hours. Most impulse urges disappear on their own.

## 3. Automate your savings envelope first

Treat savings like a fixed expense. The moment income arrives, move it to your savings envelope. What's left is what you have to spend.

## 4. Track small purchases

Coffee, convenience stores, apps — these are the budget leaks that surprise everyone. For one week, log every single transaction. The patterns are always illuminating.

## 5. Review subscriptions quarterly

Subscriptions auto-renew quietly. Set a quarterly reminder to audit what you're paying for and cancel anything you haven't used in 60 days.
