Features

Everything you need to
master your money

Built on the proven envelope method. Zero-based budgeting that actually works.

Zero-based budgeting

Assign every rupee of income to an envelope before you spend it. Income minus allocations always equals zero.

Envelope budgeting

Virtual envelopes for every spending category. Move money between envelopes when plans change.

Multi-account support

Link savings, checking, and credit card accounts under one budget. Track across all of them in real-time.

Goals & savings tracking

Set savings targets for vacations, emergency funds, or purchases. Watch your progress month over month.

Privacy first

Your financial data stays yours. Self-host on your own server. No ads, no analytics, no data brokering.

Open source (GPL v3) Free forever

Fully auditable. Contribute features. Fork it. Run it anywhere. The code is on GitHub right now.
