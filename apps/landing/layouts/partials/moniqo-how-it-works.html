How it works

Three steps to
financial clarity

Moniqo makes zero-based envelope budgeting intuitive, visual, and stress-free.

1

Enter your income

Add your salary, freelance earnings, or any money that comes in. Moniqo puts it in your "To Be Budgeted" pool.

2

Fill your envelopes

Allocate every rupee to named envelopes: Rent, Groceries, Fun, Emergency Fund. When the envelope is empty, stop spending.

3

Track & adjust

Record transactions against envelopes. Move money between envelopes when life happens. Your budget reflects reality, not a spreadsheet.

Inspired by YNAB, built open-source so your data stays yours.
