01

Getting Started

Moniqo is built around one idea: every rupee you have should be assigned a purpose before you spend it. This is the zero-based budgeting principle. Once you see it working, you will never want to budget any other way.

Before you open Moniqo, have these ready:

1

Your current account balances

Check your bank app or passbook for today's balance in each account.

2

Your monthly income figure

Use your take-home pay, not gross. If income is irregular, use a conservative average.

3

A rough list of your spending categories

Rent, groceries, transport, utilities, eating out — whatever applies to you.

02

Create Your Budget

A budget in Moniqo is your financial workspace. Think of it as a single household's finances. Most people need just one budget. If you manage money separately from a partner or want to track a side project independently, you can create a second budget.

Tap "New Budget" from the home screen

Give it a name like "My Budget" or "Family Budget 2026". You can rename it later.

Select the month you want to start from

You can start mid-month. Use whatever cash you have available today as your starting point.

Your budget is created and ready to fill

The "To Be Budgeted" figure shows how much money you have left to assign. Aim to reach zero.

Tip: Do not try to make your first budget perfect. You will adjust it after the first week once you see how you actually spend.

03

Add Accounts

Accounts in Moniqo represent real-world financial accounts: your savings account, current account, cash wallet, or credit card. Moniqo does not connect to your bank, you enter balances and transactions manually. This keeps your data private and makes you more conscious of every transaction.

Account type Example Starting balance
Savings SBI Savings 4521 Current balance in your passbook
Current HDFC Current 8831 Current balance in your bank app
Credit card Axis Flipkart Card Outstanding balance as a negative number
Cash Wallet Notes and coins you currently hold

Note: For credit cards, enter the outstanding balance as a negative starting balance. As you record purchases, the balance will grow. Your credit card envelope should cover the payment when it is due.

04

Set Up Envelopes

Envelopes are named spending categories. You allocate a portion of your income to each envelope at the start of the month. When you spend, money leaves the envelope. The goal is that by the 31st, every envelope is at zero or slightly positive.

05

Record Transactions

Every purchase or income event becomes a transaction in Moniqo. Recording transactions manually takes under ten seconds once you are used to it, and the habit of noting every expense is itself a powerful behaviour change.

Example: Recording a grocery purchase
Date 2 June 2026
Payee Big Bazaar
Envelope Groceries
Account HDFC Current 8831
Amount - ₹1,340

After saving the transaction, your Groceries envelope balance drops by ₹1,340 and your HDFC account balance drops by the same amount. Both stay in sync automatically.

06

End of Month

At the end of each month, Moniqo helps you roll over your budget. Any unspent money in an envelope can be carried forward into the next month, moved to a savings goal, or returned to "To Be Budgeted".

Carry forward surplus

Leftover grocery money rolls into next month's grocery envelope by default. It builds a small buffer.

Move surplus to a goal

Redirecting ₹500 of unspent eating-out money to your vacation goal is one click.

Return to "To Be Budgeted"

Useful when an envelope consistently has too much allocated. Reallocate it more accurately next month.
